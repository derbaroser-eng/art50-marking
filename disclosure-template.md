# Deployer Disclosure Template - Art. 50(4) AI Act

Fill the bracketed fields and present this notice to natural persons exposed
to the synthetic output. The text notice complements (does not replace) the
machine-readable marking required by Art. 50(2).

Engineering template, not legal advice. Have counsel review the final wording.

---

**Notice - artificially generated or manipulated content**

This [image / audio recording / video / text] was artificially generated or
manipulated by [SYSTEM NAME], an AI system provided by [PROVIDER NAME].

The content carries a machine-readable mark identifying it as artificially
generated (IPTC DigitalSourceType: trainedAlgorithmicMedia), embedded as XMP
metadata for images or as a sha256-bound JSON sidecar for other formats.

Questions about this content: [CONTACT EMAIL].

This notice is provided under Art. 50(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act).

---

## Filling guide

- [PROVIDER NAME]: legal entity placing the system on the market / putting it
  into service under its own name.
- [SYSTEM NAME]: the product or model name the user interacts with.
- [CONTACT EMAIL]: a monitored mailbox for content questions.
- Keep the media type that matches the output; delete the others.
- Show the notice where the user meets the output (caption, label, footer,
  interstitial before playback), not only in terms of service.
